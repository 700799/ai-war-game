# ROAD TO SINGULARITY — Superintelligence Wargame

**Reach the future. Don’t break the world.**

The *singularity* is the moment machines become smarter than people and change everything. This game asks: can you get there by 2030 without the world falling apart?

A think-tank-style, game-theoretic superintelligence wargame in a pixel/Nintendo UI. Play 4, 6 or 8 rounds against a fictional rival state, while profit-driven labs, the open-source commons, criminal syndicates and lawmakers play their own games.

**Play:** https://700799.github.io/ai-war-game/

## How a game unfolds

The map is the main screen. A console panel above it carries the briefing, crises, your choices and results, two or three buttons at a time; details open in drawers. Each round can unlock a new **world**, a small map of 3–4 buildings, and you can switch between unlocked worlds or look back at earlier rounds in History:

1. **The Capital:** Command Center, Grid Defense, Frontier Labs. Stance and reciprocity.
2. **The Home Front:** Town Hall and Robotics. Feeds, jobs, robots and fairness.
3. **The Shadows:** Security Ops and Crime. Attribution, insiders, scams.
4. **The Supply Lines:** Allies and Fabs & Power. Export controls, retaliation, tokenomics.
5. **The Model Vault:** Red Team Lab and Open Commons. Theft, distillation, poisoning, jailbreaks.

Beginner shows 4 meters, medium 5, advanced 8; the rest live in a drawer. Your rival, The Directorate, strikes harder as its threat level climbs. Byte the advisor dog weighs pros and cons when asked, but never picks for you.

## Disclaimer

ROAD TO SINGULARITY is a fictional, educational simulation about policy trade-offs around superintelligence, cybersecurity and society. It is not a prediction of real events.

- **No real countries.** The rival is an invented state and does not represent any real nation, government or people.
- **No real people or organizations.** All characters, agencies, labs and groups are fictional archetypes. The game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, think tank, university or company.
- **Simplified on purpose.** All numbers, probabilities, dates and outcomes are illustrative game mechanics, not research findings or forecasts.
- **Not advice.** Nothing here is legal, financial, security, medical or policy advice.
- **High-level only.** Harms are described in general terms for discussion; the game contains no real techniques or instructions.
